# Appointment, Cancellation, & Refund Policies

## Appointment Booking & Confirmation
- Appointments can be booked through the Patient Portal, AI Chatbot, or by calling Customer Care at +1 (800) 555-CARE.
- Upon booking, patients receive an instant confirmation notification with an Appointment ID (e.g., `APT-10023`), doctor name, date, time, and department location.

## Appointment Rescheduling Policy
- Appointments can be rescheduled online via the Customer Dashboard or AI Chatbot up to **2 hours** prior to the appointment time.
- To reschedule:
  1. Open the AI Chatbot or Customer Dashboard -> My Appointments.
  2. Select your active appointment.
  3. Pick a new available date and time slot.
  4. Confirmation of the updated slot will be issued immediately.
- Rescheduling requested within 2 hours of the slot may require support team review or contacting the clinic reception directly.

## Appointment Cancellation Policy
- Cancellations made **at least 4 hours before** the appointment time qualify for a **100% full refund** or 100% credit towards a future consultation.
- Cancellations made **between 2 to 4 hours before** the slot incur a 10% administrative processing fee (90% refund).
- Cancellations within 2 hours or no-shows are non-refundable unless emergency medical documentation is provided.

## Refund Process & Timeline
- Once a refund request is approved, the funds are automatically initiated to the original payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, UPI, or Bank Transfer).
- **Processing Timeframe**:
  - Credit/Debit Cards: 3 to 5 business days.
  - Net Banking / UPI / Direct Transfer: 1 to 3 business days.
  - MetroHealth Digital Wallet: Instant (within 1 hour).
- If your refund status shows "Processed" but money is not reflected in your bank account after 5 business days, please check with your issuing bank or submit a complaint via our support system. Delayed refund queries will be assigned to Billing Support with HIGH priority.
