# Hospital Information, Locations, & Operational Timings

## Overview
MetroHealth Medical Center is a premier multi-specialty healthcare institution dedicated to providing compassionate, high-quality patient care. Our facilities offer round-the-clock emergency medical services, outpatient clinics, diagnostic imaging, and inpatient departments.

## Operational Timings
- **Outpatient Department (OPD)**: Monday to Saturday: 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM. Sunday: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM.
- **Emergency Care & ICU**: 24/7, 365 Days a Year.
- **Diagnostic Labs & Radiology**: Monday to Saturday: 7:00 AM – 9:00 PM. Sunday: 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM. Emergency diagnostic services run 24/7.
- **Pharmacy**: Main hospital branch pharmacy operates 24/7. Outpatient clinic pharmacy opens 8:00 AM – 9:00 PM daily.
