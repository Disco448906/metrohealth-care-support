# Billing, Payments, & Insurance Guidelines

## Accepted Payment Methods
MetroHealth accepts major Credit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, AMEX), Debit Cards, Digital Wallet payments, Direct ACH Bank Transfers, and Cash at clinic billing desks.

## Invoices & Receipts
- Detailed itemized medical bills and receipts are generated automatically upon completion of consultations, diagnostic lab tests, or inpatient discharges.
- Digital invoices are accessible in the Patient Portal under the **Bills & Invoices** tab.

## Common Billing Discrepancies & Complaints
- **Double Charges or Extra Fees**: If you observe duplicate charges or incorrect fee calculations on your invoice, notify our AI Chatbot or Customer Service.
- If the AI Chatbot detects a billing discrepancy (e.g., wrong billing amount, unapplied discount, duplicate transaction), it creates an official support ticket (Category: `billing`, Priority: `HIGH`) assigned to the Billing & Accounts Department.

## Insurance Coverage & Claims
- MetroHealth is empaneled with major health insurance providers (e.g., Aetna, BlueCross BlueShield, Cigna, UnitedHealth, Medicare, Alliance).
- **Cashless Treatment**: Available for inpatient procedures when pre-authorization approval is submitted at least 48 hours prior to planned admission.
- **Reimbursement Claims**: For outpatient diagnostic tests or consultations, submit your itemized invoice along with the doctor's prescription and claim form to your insurer.
- **Disputes**: If an insurance claim is rejected or disputed due to missing hospital paperwork, submit an **Insurance Claim Dispute** ticket through our system. Our insurance desk will review the documentation within 2 business days.
