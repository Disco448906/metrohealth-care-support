# Hospital Departments & Doctor Availability

## Departments & Specialties
MetroHealth Medical Center houses specialized medical departments:
1. **Cardiology**: Heart health, ECG, Echocardiogram, Angiography, Pacemakers.
2. **Neurology & Neurosurgery**: Brain & nerve disorders, EEG, Brain MRI.
3. **Orthopedics**: Bone, joint, fracture, spine, and joint replacement surgery.
4. **Pediatrics**: Child healthcare, vaccinations, pediatric emergency care.
5. **General Internal Medicine**: General health consults, chronic disease management (diabetes, hypertension).
6. **Dermatology & Allergy**: Skin conditions, allergy testing, cosmetic dermatology.
7. **Oncology**: Cancer diagnosis, chemotherapy, radiation therapy.
8. **Obstetrics & Gynecology**: Maternal care, labor & delivery, routine gynecological exams.
