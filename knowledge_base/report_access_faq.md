# Medical Reports, Prescriptions, & Portal FAQ

## Accessing Medical Reports & Prescriptions
- Lab test results (Blood tests, Pathology) are published to the Patient Portal within 12 to 24 hours of sample collection.
- Advanced Imaging Reports (MRI, CT Scan, X-Ray) are published within 24 to 48 hours following radiologist review.
- Electronic Prescriptions written during consultations are instantly added to your patient record under **My Prescriptions**.

## Downloading Reports
1. Log into your MetroHealth account via the web portal or mobile app.
2. Navigate to **Records & Reports**.
3. Click **Download PDF** next to the desired diagnostic test or doctor prescription.

## Account & Login Troubleshooting
- **Forgot Password**: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page to receive a password reset link via SMS or email.
- **Account Locked**: If your account is locked after multiple incorrect password attempts, wait 15 minutes or contact support for password assistance.
- **Incorrect Patient Data / Reports**: If you notice errors in your medical report or demographic details (e.g., misspelled name, wrong age), do NOT edit it yourself. Submit an **Incorrect Medical Report** or **Incorrect Patient Information** request via the Chatbot to issue a support ticket.

## Escalation & Complaint Policy
- Routine queries (Timings, Doctor lists, Cancellation rules) are answered instantly by the AI system (`AUTO_RESOLVE`).
- Operational or billing issues trigger ticket creation (`CREATE_TICKET`) with a unique Ticket ID format (`MED-2026-XXXXX`).
- **Critical Safety Escalations**: Complaints regarding medication errors, serious patient safety issues, or privacy breaches receive immediate `CRITICAL_ESCALATION`, generating a CRITICAL ticket for urgent management review and providing emergency contact information to the patient.
